## §2 Q3. What is the weak-card protocol?

Next. Q3. What is the weak-card protocol? Answer. 45–60 min cap. (1) Open gotchas + Mock #4 three fix-forwards. (2) Pull only matching cards. (3) Round 1: front → say back → check. (4) Round 2: misses only. (5) Box closed — no third round. Follow-ups. Fix-forward not on card?: Write one index card front — don’t build new deck.. Whole deck temptation?: Violates taper — chronic gotchas + 3 fixes only.. Miss same card twice?: Round 2 only — if still miss, one line on index card; stop..
