# Audio script — Sample 01 — STAR rhythm & prompt router (Q&A)
> Listen-only sample Q&A from `01-star-rhythm.md`. Spoken answers and follow-ups.

## §0 Q1. What is the STAR timing rhythm?

Next. Q1. What is the STAR timing rhythm? Answer. Situation + Task ~20s — scale and your ownership, not company history. Action ~90s — 3–5 concrete steps plus one trade-off; say what you did. Result ~30s — one real metric or crisp outcome. Lesson ~20s — reusable principle, not a humble-brag essay. Total target: 2–3 minutes. Leadership means ownership clarity, not title theater. Follow-ups. What if they want more detail?: Offer one follow-up lane — don’t expand the whole STAR past 4 min.. Can Action be shorter?: Yes if Result is strong — but don’t skip the trade-off in Action.. Is “Task” separate from Situation?: Often combined in ~20s — “here’s the context and what I owned.”.

## §1 Q2. How do I open a behavioral answer?

Next. Q2. How do I open a behavioral answer? Answer. Lead with the agenda: “I’ll take ~2 minutes on [X] — context, what I owned, outcome.” That signals clock discipline and gives the interviewer permission to interrupt cleanly. Use the same opener for conflict (S6), incident (S 8), AI judgment (S9), and hard technical (S 1). Follow-ups. What if they say “keep it brief”?: Aim for 90s compressed — S/T + one Action step + Result + Lesson.. Should I ask “is that enough detail?”: No — state your plan and execute; they’ll redirect if needed.. Behavioral pulled mid-mock?: Same opener — “~2 minutes — context, what I owned, outcome.”.

## §2 Q3. How does the prompt → story router work?

Next. Q3. How does the prompt → story router work? Answer. Map the prompt to a story ID in ≤10 seconds: Hard technical → S 1 (Ads/HeroWidget). Conflict → S6 (LE sheet) or S 8 (I M O C). Incident / on-call → S 8. Mentorship without title → S6, S 10, S14, or S 1. AI / productivity tools → S9 (District) — contrast S15 if product AI. Why senior / impact → S6 30%+, S 8 99.95%, 30L+ daily active users. Failure → real miss + process change — never a fake hero failure. Follow-ups. Two stories fit — which wins?: Pick the one with the strongest metric and clearest your ownership.. Privacy / on-device AI asked?: S15 or S16 — not S9.. Backup if S 1 feels thin?: S 10 for S D K boundaries..

## §3 Q4. What is the metric trio?

Next. Q4. What is the metric trio? Answer. One clean sentence you can say anywhere: “I’ve owned features on BookMyShow at 30L+ daily active users, helped drive 99.95%+ crash-free discipline on my paths, and shipped UX like the LE bottom sheet that cut full-screen navigations by 30%+ on targeted flows.” Use it in “tell me about yourself,” “biggest impact,” and “why senior” — always with honest scope wording. Follow-ups. Can I lead with only one metric?: Yes — pick S6 nav or S 8 reliability depending on the question.. “Helped drive” vs “owned”?: Honest: you participated in crash-free culture; don’t claim sole crash free sessions ownership.. 30L+ daily active users — say lakh or million?: Either works — “30+ lakh daily active users” or “30L+ daily active users” if your resume uses it..

## §4 Q5. How do I separate AI tooling from product AI?

Next. Q5. How do I separate AI tooling from product AI? Answer. One breath: “District S9 is how I use AI as an engineer — context and review. FinTrack/GymFlow are product on-device systems. I don’t conflate them.” S9 = Cursor/Copilot acceleration inside module boundaries and a review bar. S15/S16 = privacy-first on-device inference, RAG, fail-soft — only if they ask product AI. Follow-ups. “Did AI write your code?”: No — tools accelerated boilerplate/tests; I own architecture and reject bad output.. When they pivot S9 → S15?: Acknowledge the distinction, then answer the product story.. Tool-worship red flag?: “AI makes me 10×” with no review bar — senior answer names context engineering..

## §5 Q6. What should I NOT do in a STAR?

Next. Q6. What should I NOT do in a STAR? Answer. Don’t run past 4 minutes — looks junior. Don’t say “we” for ten sentences without your role. Don’t invent metrics (fill-rate %, company-wide nav −30%). Don’t turn conflict into winning a taste argument. Don’t claim sole credit for company-wide crash-free. Don’t use a humblebrag “failure.” Don’t conflate S9 tooling with S15 product AI. Follow-ups. “We collaborated well” — enough?: No — need contract, your steps, and a metric or crisp outcome.. Can I dramatize an incident?: Process beats drama — stabilize, communicate, prevent.. Opinions vs data in conflict?: Contracts and user impact over opinions..

## §6 Q7. How long should “tell me about yourself” be?

Next. Q7. How long should “tell me about yourself” be? Answer. 90–120 seconds — one breath per career chapter, not four full STARs. Spine: BookMyShow scale + reliability + delivery metrics → District architecture + AI judgment → Raw S D K/hybrid → FinTrack/GymFlow → target senior ownership. End with what you want next: senior roles owning architecture, reliability, and delivery end-to-end. Follow-ups. Include every project?: No — hit scale proof, one specialty, and the senior goal.. Repeat full S6 inside TMAY?: No — cite 30%+ in one clause; save the full STAR for behavioral.. Too long at 3 min?: Cut side projects; keep BMS + District + senior target..
