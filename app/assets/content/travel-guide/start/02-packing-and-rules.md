# Packing list and 90-minute rules

## Carry

- This app, already offline.
- Earphones if you **Listen**; **Read** is default on web.
- Paper or Notes for one layer-stack drawing.
- Laptop is optional. A 90-minute ride is mostly speaking, not Xcode.

## Rules

1. **One trip chapter per direction.** 60–90 minutes. Then stop.
2. **Speak answers.** Reading is not encoding.
3. **One** of the 30 questions per ride, not ten.
4. If you cannot say the parenthesis, re-read that sentence — do not search outside.
5. Archived weekly Q&A is **Library**, not today’s spine.

## Mumbai–Pune notes

- **Expressway:** passenger only.
- **Train:** ticket checker is a pause, not a reason to open Twitter.
- **Same-day return:** Trip A out (~75 min), Trip B back (~75 min). That is a full week of system-design reps.
- **Late night:** skip overflow. Sleep beats a second question.
