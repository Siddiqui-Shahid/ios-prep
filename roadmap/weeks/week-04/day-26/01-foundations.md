# 01 — Foundations: STAR Rhythm & Prompt Router

---

## 0. North star

**Situation/Task 20s → Action 90s (3–5 concrete steps + one trade-off) → Result 30s (real metric or crisp outcome) → Lesson 20s. Leadership = ownership clarity, not title theater.**

---

## 1. Timing table

| Segment | Time | Do | Don’t |
|---|---|---|---|
| Situation + Task | ~20s | Scale + your ownership | Company history lecture |
| Action | ~90s | 3–5 steps, one trade-off | “We” without your role |
| Result | ~30s | Metric or crisp outcome | Fake numbers |
| Lesson | ~20s | Reusable principle | Humble-brag essay |

**Opener:**  
> “I’ll take ~2 minutes on [X] — context, what I owned, outcome.”

---

## 2. Prompt → story router (≤10s)

| Interviewer asks | Reach for | Backup |
|---|---|---|
| Hard technical project | **S1** Ads/HeroWidget | S10 |
| Conflict / disagreement | **S6** LE sheet **or** **S8** IMOC | S14 |
| Incident / on-call | **S8** | S2 |
| Mentorship / leadership sans title | S6, S10, S14, **S1** | — |
| AI / productivity tools | **S9** District | contrast S15 |
| Privacy / on-device AI | S15 | S16 |
| Why senior / impact | S6 **30%+**, S8 **99.95%**, **30L+ DAU** | — |
| Failure | Real miss + process change | No fake hero failure |

---

## 3. Metric trio (say cleanly)

> “I’ve owned features on BookMyShow at **30L+ DAU**, helped drive **99.95%+ crash-free** discipline on my paths, and shipped UX like the LE bottom sheet that cut full-screen navigations by **30%+** on targeted flows.”

---

## 4. AI tooling vs product AI (one breath)

> “District S9 is how I use AI as an engineer — context and review. FinTrack/GymFlow are product on-device systems. I don’t conflate them.”
