# Drill — think out loud (DSA / GSA)

**From:** 28 Aug. Interviewer: they are **not** judging syntax memorization; they want a **one-on-one developer conversation**. They invited algorithms even without perfect Swift. That is a hint you were going quiet or jumping to code.

**DSA** (Data Structures and Algorithms: arrays, graphs, complexity). **GSA** here means **general system awareness** (why O(n), why a cursor, why not poll).

## Agenda they want to hear (90s)

1. Restate the problem in one sentence.
2. Example + edge (empty, one element, duplicates).
3. Brute force + complexity.
4. Better idea (two pointers, hash, sort).
5. Then code or pseudocode.
6. Test the example out loud.

## Line to open every DSA prompt

> “I’ll clarify, then brute force, then improve, then code. Stop me if you want a different path.”
