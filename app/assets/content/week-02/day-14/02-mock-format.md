### Q1. What is Mock #2’s block structure and timing?

**Points to:** [Foundations · §3 Mock #2 format](../01-foundations.md#3-mock-2-format-memorize) · [Deep dive · §1 Facilitator script](../02-deep-dive.md#1-facilitator-script--mock-2-self-or-peer)

**Answer:**

> **Warm-ups:** 15–20 min, 4–5 Normal mixed Week 2. **Tricky:** 15–20 min, 2 deep items. **Architecture talk:** **5:00 hard stop** — Ads **or** SDUI (pick one). **STAR:** 10 min — S1 or S3/S12 + spice S4/S9. **Retro:** 10–15 min — score timing, log gotchas tagged `week-02`. Full script in [`02-deep-dive.md`](../02-deep-dive.md) and [`code/MockTalkTracks.md`](../code/MockTalkTracks.md).

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|

| Self mode? | Timer visible; record voice memo; score 1–5. |
| Pick both tracks? | **Forbidden** for 5-min talk — one crisp, one skim after. |

### Q4. How does the 5-minute architecture talk timing work?

**Points to:** [Foundations · §5 Timing muscle](../01-foundations.md#5-timing-muscle-architecture) · [Deep dive · Block 3](../02-deep-dive.md#block-3--architecture-talk-500-hard-stop)

**Answer:**

> 0:00–0:20 agenda + scope. 0:20–1:00 problem/context. 1:00–3:30 architecture beats (3–4). 3:30–4:30 failure modes / trade-offs. 4:30–5:00 stop; invite questions. **Hard rule:** if you hit 5:00 mid-sentence, stop and invite questions — re-record if over 5:30.

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|
| Track choice? | Declare Track A (Ads) or B (SDUI) at setup. |
| Record separately? | Yes — architecture block as its own take. |
| Failure modes optional? | Mention if time — don’t skip agenda for detail. |

### Q7. Why pick ONE primary architecture track?

**Points to:** [Foundations · §4 Choose ONE primary track](../01-foundations.md#4-choose-one-primary-track) · [Deep dive · §6 Trade-offs meta mock](../02-deep-dive.md#6-trade-offs-meta-mock)

**Answer:**

> POP/HeroWidget/pinning → **Track A Ads**. Schema/fallback/header/splash → **Track B SDUI**. Doing both cold → neither talk crisp. Skim the other track ~20 min **after** your recording. Ego choice of “both” costs the 5-min spine quality.

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|
| Stronger on both? | Still pick one for timed recording; alternate next practice. |
| Bridge answer? | SDUI config + native HeroWidget renderer — best of both in Q&A, not one 5-min mashup. |
