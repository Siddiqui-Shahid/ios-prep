# Audio script — How this handbook works

## §0 Introduction

How this handbook works This is one book. Not a dashboard. You always have a place: a part , a chapter , a page . The table of contents on the cover is the spine. Finish a page. Mark it complete. Come back. The next page is the next page.

## §1 Two doors, same hallway

Read in order. Open Session 1, then Session 2, and so on. Each session is 60–90 minutes of this book — the time you actually have on a Mumbai–Pune ride. The train is how you use the book. It is not the product. Jump by topic. Open Part III if you want lists. Open Part V if they scheduled a system-design round tomorrow. You still land on a numbered page with a previous and a next. The old four-week dump lives in Archive. It is not the spine.

## §2 Voice

I will sit next to you and talk like a coach. When a KEYWORD shows up, a plain explanation sits in parentheses. If they ask X, say Y. Not a CMS of tiles.

## §3 What you will cover

Part II is the weak spots from your recorded rounds (KT ignored). Drill until you can speak them. Part III is iOS craft: memory, lists, networking, production, communication, DSA think-aloud. Part IV is how to run LLD and HLD in 45 minutes. Part V is full sample system designs — problem → requirements → API → iOS LLD → HLD → tradeoffs → a 60-second close you can speak. Part VI is the 30 machine-design prompts, short and speakable. Part VII maps sessions to chapters so you know what to read on this ride.

## §4 Quality bar

If a page is a bullet salad, it failed. Prefer a short story with an example: a feed cell, a Pay button, a 1% crash. Mark complete only when you can explain the page out loud without looking.
