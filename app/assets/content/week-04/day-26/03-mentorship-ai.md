> “I’ll use a real miss, not a humblebrag. We under-specified pin rotation and break-glass readiness on a high-traffic networking path. Impact was elevated TLS failure risk and fire-drill ops when pins aged. I owned the fix and changed process: documented backup-pin / staged-rollout thinking (**Design: pin rotation / break-glass (not shipped runbook)** design), added monitoring on TLS failure rate, and made break-glass ownership explicit in the runbook. I know it won’t recur because detection sits in metrics and the checklist gates widen — not because we ‘tried harder.’”
