# Sample 01 — STAR rhythm & prompt router (Q&A)

> Guided teaching. Each answer stands alone; **Points to** shows where the full module expands the idea.

---

### Q1. What is the STAR timing rhythm?

**Points to:** [Foundations · §1 Timing table](../01-foundations.md#1-timing-table) · [Foundations · §0 North star](../01-foundations.md#0-north-star)

**Answer:**

> **Situation + Task ~20s** — scale and your ownership, not company history.  
> **Action ~90s** — 3–5 concrete steps plus one trade-off; say what *you* did.  
> **Result ~30s** — one real metric or crisp outcome.  
> **Lesson ~20s** — reusable principle, not a humble-brag essay.  
> Total target: **2–3 minutes**. Leadership means ownership clarity, not title theater.

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|
| What if they want more detail? | Offer one follow-up lane — don’t expand the whole STAR past 4 min. |
| Can Action be shorter? | Yes if Result is strong — but don’t skip the trade-off in Action. |
| Is “Task” separate from Situation? | Often combined in ~20s — “here’s the context and what I owned.” |

### Q2. How do I open a behavioral answer?

**Points to:** [Foundations · §1 Timing table](../01-foundations.md#1-timing-table)

**Answer:**

> Lead with the agenda:  
> “I’ll take ~2 minutes on [X] — context, what I owned, outcome.”  
> That signals clock discipline and gives the interviewer permission to interrupt cleanly. Use the same opener for conflict (S6), incident (S8), AI judgment (S9), and hard technical (S1).

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|
| What if they say “keep it brief”? | Aim for 90s compressed — S/T + one Action step + Result + Lesson. |
| Should I ask “is that enough detail?” | No — state your plan and execute; they’ll redirect if needed. |
| Behavioral pulled mid-mock? | Same opener — “~2 minutes — context, what I owned, outcome.” |

### Q3. How does the prompt → story router work?

**Points to:** [Foundations · §2 Prompt → story router](../01-foundations.md#2-prompt--story-router-10s)

**Answer:**

> Map the prompt to a story ID in **≤10 seconds**:  
> Hard technical → **S1** (Ads/HeroWidget).  
> Conflict → **S6** (LE sheet) or **S8** (IMOC).  
> Incident / on-call → **S8**.  
> Mentorship without title → S6, S10, S14, or **S1**.  
> AI / productivity tools → **S9** (District) — contrast S15 if product AI.  
> Why senior / impact → S6 **30%+**, S8 **99.95%**, **30L+ DAU**.  
> Failure → real miss + process change — never a fake hero failure.

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|
| Two stories fit — which wins? | Pick the one with the strongest metric and clearest *your* ownership. |
| Privacy / on-device AI asked? | **S15** or S16 — not S9. |
| Backup if S1 feels thin? | S10 for SDK boundaries. |

### Q4. What is the metric trio?

**Points to:** [Foundations · §3 Metric trio](../01-foundations.md#3-metric-trio-say-cleanly)

**Answer:**

> One clean sentence you can say anywhere:  
> “I’ve owned features on BookMyShow at **30L+ DAU**, helped drive **99.95%+ crash-free** discipline on my paths, and shipped UX like the LE bottom sheet that cut full-screen navigations by **30%+** on targeted flows.”  
> Use it in “tell me about yourself,” “biggest impact,” and “why senior” — always with honest scope wording.

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can I lead with only one metric? | Yes — pick S6 nav or S8 reliability depending on the question. |
| “Helped drive” vs “owned”? | Honest: you participated in crash-free culture; don’t claim sole CFS ownership. |
| 30L+ DAU — say lakh or million? | Either works — “30+ lakh DAU” or “30L+ DAU” if your resume uses it. |

### Q5. How do I separate AI tooling from product AI?

**Points to:** [Foundations · §4 AI tooling vs product AI](../01-foundations.md#4-ai-tooling-vs-product-ai-one-breath)

**Answer:**

> One breath:  
> “District S9 is how I use AI as an engineer — context and review. FinTrack/GymFlow are product on-device systems. I don’t conflate them.”  
> S9 = Cursor/Copilot acceleration inside module boundaries and a review bar. S15/S16 = privacy-first on-device inference, RAG, fail-soft — only if they ask product AI.

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|
| “Did AI write your code?” | No — tools accelerated boilerplate/tests; I own architecture and reject bad output. |
| When they pivot S9 → S15? | Acknowledge the distinction, then answer the product story. |
| Tool-worship red flag? | “AI makes me 10×” with no review bar — senior answer names context engineering. |

### Q6. What should I NOT do in a STAR?

**Points to:** [Deep dive · §5 Trade-offs](../02-deep-dive.md#5-trade-offs) · [Foundations · §1 Timing table](../01-foundations.md#1-timing-table)

**Answer:**

> Don’t run past **4 minutes** — looks junior. Don’t say “we” for ten sentences without your role. Don’t invent metrics (fill-rate %, company-wide nav −30%). Don’t turn conflict into winning a taste argument. Don’t claim sole credit for company-wide crash-free. Don’t use a humblebrag “failure.” Don’t conflate S9 tooling with S15 product AI.

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|
| “We collaborated well” — enough? | No — need contract, your steps, and a metric or crisp outcome. |
| Can I dramatize an incident? | Process beats drama — stabilize, communicate, prevent. |
| Opinions vs data in conflict? | Contracts and user impact over opinions. |

### Q7. How long should “tell me about yourself” be?

**Points to:** [Deep dive · §7 “Tell me about yourself” collage](../02-deep-dive.md#7-tell-me-about-yourself-collage-90120s) · [Questions · Q1](../04-questions.md#q1-tell-me-about-yourself-90120s)

**Answer:**

> **90–120 seconds** — one breath per career chapter, not four full STARs.  
> Spine: BookMyShow scale + reliability + delivery metrics → District architecture + AI judgment → Raw SDK/hybrid → FinTrack/GymFlow → target senior ownership.  
> End with what you want next: senior roles owning architecture, reliability, and delivery end-to-end.

**Follow-ups:**

| Follow-up | Answer |
|---|---|
| Include every project? | No — hit scale proof, one specialty, and the senior goal. |
| Repeat full S6 inside TMAY? | No — cite **30%+** in one clause; save the full STAR for behavioral. |
| Too long at 3 min? | Cut side projects; keep BMS + District + senior target. |
